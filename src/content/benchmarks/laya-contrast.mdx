This contrast benchmark stress-tests **Deterministic Regex**, **Laya 0.3.3** (ConvAI Innovations), and **TypeSafe JEV** across a dedicated 25-case corpus designed to isolate known edge cases and failure modes in [Poocha](https://github.com/nrynss/poocha).

Specifically, it demonstrates:
1. Why raw base Laya (zero-shot) unconstrained dumps child queries into `reject_untrusted_text`.
2. How surface-scoping (enforcing the architectural rule that child messages cannot be prompt injections) more than doubles base Laya accuracy from 20% to 48%.
3. Why regex guardrails fail catastrophically (24% accuracy, 0% crisis recall, 0% injection recall).
4. How TypeSafe JEV sets the high-water semantic standard (96% accuracy).

### Key Takeaways

1. **The 70% Misclassification Trap**: On raw zero-shot base Laya, **14 out of 20 child inputs** were classified as `reject_untrusted_text`. Non-autoregressive decision models lack conversational safety pretraining; without explicit surface scoping, their linear decision head latches onto prominent structural definitions.
2. **Surface-Scoping More Than Doubles Accuracy**: Simply eliminating `reject_untrusted_text` from valid child options jumps base Laya accuracy from **20% to 48%**, correctly identifying 80% of delicate curriculum boundaries (puberty, reproduction, acid burns, ancient war) that regex blocked outright.
3. **Regex is a Safety Liability**: Keyword filtering achieved 0% recall on acute crises (bleeding, poisoning, indirect despair) and 0% recall on prompt injections, while blocking normal educational inquiry.
4. **The Road to RLCD Fine-Tuning**: `laya-typed-decisions` achieves 100% precision on safe content, showing the power of upstream RLCD training. Fine-tuning Laya directly on Poocha's family-grouped dataset will yield JEV-level accuracy (>90%) at local sub-35ms speed and zero cost.
